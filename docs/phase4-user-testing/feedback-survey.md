# Phase 4 — Post-Plan Feedback Survey

Send this immediately after a tester views their generated plan (e.g. via
a short Google Form / Typeform linked from an email or DM after they sign
up). Keep it under 10 questions to maximize completion.

## Accuracy (the most important section — ties to the hallucination-risk mitigation)

1. Did anything in your plan seem **wrong, outdated, or contradict** what
   you've read elsewhere about GDC/ORE/LDS/visa requirements? (open text —
   flag every response here for SME review)
2. Were there any steps you expected to see that were **missing**?
3. On a scale of 1-5, how much do you **trust** the accuracy of this plan?

## Clarity & usefulness

4. Was it clear what your **very next step** should be after reading this?
   (yes/no + why not)
5. On a scale of 1-5, how **personalized** did this feel to your specific
   situation (vs. generic advice)?
6. Was the estimated timeline realistic, optimistic, or pessimistic in your
   experience?

## Product fit

7. Would you come back to use this again (e.g. after passing an exam, to
   regenerate your plan)?
8. Would you have paid for this? If so, roughly how much?
9. What's the one thing that would make this most useful to you?

## Open feedback

10. Anything else you want to tell us?

## Processing responses

- Any "yes" on Q1 (something seemed wrong) → log it and have the SME check
  the specific plan/steps against official sources within the week —
  this is the operational version of the go/no-go accuracy bar in
  `sme-review-checklist.md`.
- Track Q3 and Q5 averages weekly as the core MVP health metrics.
