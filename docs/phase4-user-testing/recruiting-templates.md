# Phase 4 — Recruiting Templates (30-50 dentist test cohort)

Target: 30-50 foreign-trained dentists at any stage of the UK pathway.
Start recruiting in parallel with the build (around week 6) — recruiting
alone often takes 2-3 weeks even with a warm network.

## Where to post

- The SME's personal network (most credible source — lead with this)
- Foreign-trained dentist Facebook/WhatsApp groups focused on UK GDC/ORE
- Relevant LinkedIn groups/hashtags (#ORE, #GDCregistration, #UKdentist)
- Dental forums where this audience already asks GDC/ORE questions

## Outreach message (community groups)

> Hi all — I'm building a free tool that turns your GDC/ORE/visa situation
> into a personalized step-by-step plan (no more guessing what to do next
> across GDC, ORE, LDS, OET/IELTS, and visa sponsorship). Looking for 30-50
> people at any stage of the UK pathway to try it and tell me honestly what
> it gets right or wrong. Takes about 5 minutes, completely free, and your
> feedback directly shapes what we build next. Comment or DM if you'd like
> early access — happy to share with anyone interested in dental
> migration to the UK.

## Direct/DM message (warmer, via SME's contacts)

> Hi [name], [SME] suggested I reach out — I'm building a tool that
> generates a personalized UK dental career roadmap (GDC/ORE/visa/job
> search) for dentists trained outside the UK, and I'd really value your
> take on it since you've [are currently / have already] navigated this.
> Would you be open to trying it and giving me 10 minutes of honest
> feedback? No cost, and it might also be directly useful to you.

## What to ask for on signup

- Confirm they fit the target (foreign-trained, exploring/pursuing UK
  registration)
- Get explicit consent that their (anonymized) feedback may inform product
  decisions

## Incentive (optional, budget-dependent)

A small thank-you (e.g. a gift card) for completing both the questionnaire
and the feedback survey can meaningfully improve response rates — budget
roughly 1,000-3,000 AED total if doing this, per the project's budget note.
